One Chip, Infinite Potential

The beating heart of the A14 is the all-new AMD Ryzen™ AI Max+ 392 processor with Radeon™ 8060S Graphics. Putting a 12-core Zen 5 CPU with 40 compute units of RDNA 3.5 graphics into a single processor, this incredible piece of silicon marries powerful processing with gaming performance that rivals a dedicated GPU. Best of all, this chip draws significantly less combined power than a standard GPU and CPU configuration, allowing the A14 to stay cooler and last longer than its competitors.

Ultra-power efficient CPU and GPU

The TUF Gaming A14 redefines the mobile experience by pairing the AMD Ryzen™ AI Max 392 processor with integrated Radeon™ 8060S graphics. This combination is designed for an optimal efficiency curve, delivering smooth performance and cooler thermals in a lightweight, easy-to-carry-around laptop. The power efficiency gains extend across the entire system, with both the CPU and GPU optimized to provide excellent results at lower wattages to help optimize battery life. When pushed, the A14 scales up to provide impressive multi-threaded performance, giving gamers, creators, and AI developers a versatile, highly portable machine that handles modern workloads with ease.

Unified memory

The A14’s unified memory system allows its ultra-high speed LPDDR5X 8000MT/s quad-channel RAM to be reallocated as needed between system RAM and VRAM. Crank up texture quality when gaming or give yourself more system memory for demanding productivity applications. Whether you like to have a ton of tabs open when surfing the web, want loads of RAM for professional video editing, or need the memory to run large data sets, the A14 is ready.

0db Ambient Cooling
Enjoy truly silent cooling under light workloads with 0dB Ambient Cooling technology. In the Silent operating mode, the cooling system switches off all fans during everyday tasks to dissipate heat passively. This lets you concentrate on your work and immerse yourself in movies with less distraction. If CPU or GPU temperatures increase, the fans automatically switch on again.
Redesigned keyboard
Previous keyboard designs focused on keeping the chassis temperatures cool, keeping your fingers comfortable during long gaming sessions. The newly redesigned keyboard not only keeps your hands comfortable on the trackpad and keyboard deck, but increases air intake for the main system components as well, boosting the A14’s cooling performance without increasing weight or chassis size.
Fluid Dynamic Bearing
Both fans have a fluid bearing design, which is a tried and tested style that will keep the A14 operating at peak performance for years to come.
97-blade fans
The dual fans come with 97 ultra-thin blades, just 0.1mm thick, enabling high airflow and static pressure without extra noise.
Inward airflow 1.0
The fan shrouds have precisely calculated cutouts to direct some airflow toward internal components like memory and processor keeping them cooler and operating at peak efficiency.
0.1mm Ultra-Thin Fins
The A14’s thermal energy is dissipated by two heatsinks, each lined with ultra-thin copper fins as slim as 0.1 mm. Higher density fins allow for increased heat dissipation and lower air resistance for smoother flow.
Dust Filter
When dust and fibers collect inside a machine, heat can get trapped, causing degraded performance over time. A filter over the A14’s air intakes helps keep dust out, allowing the CPU and GPU to breathe easier for years of smooth and stable operation.
Exhaust Vents
The TUF Gaming A14 features a large rear heatsink, making the most of its 14-inch frame. This rear exhaust keeps hot air and fan noise pointed away from you and your hands for a cooler and quieter experience.
